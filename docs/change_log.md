## Training example — Automatic customer reminder

Requested change:
Send an automatic reminder to the customer if they have not
responded within 48 hours of delivery of the additional-work proposal.

Reason:
Reduce delays and the need for manual follow-up.

Impact:
Estimated effort: 4 hours of implementation and 2 hours of testing.
Price to the customer with standard testing: PLN 900.
Earlier testing adds PLN 300, bringing the total customer price
to PLN 1.200 and allowing delivery before the planned pilot.
This exceeds the customer's previously approved limit of PLN 1.000.

Decision:
Within this training simulation, the customer approved
the total price of PLN 1.200 for implementation with earlier testing.
Proceed with the additional feature under these agreed terms.

Owner:
PM

Status:
Approved — implementation and testing not yet completed.
